# WTFact social kit: copy (English)

Captions for the graphics in `en/`. Every fact comes from a post on the site, where the sources are listed.
Rules: always write "WTFact?!" with "?!", use the slogan only next to sourced facts, never on polls or joke posts. Sources are always one tap away (link in bio).

Always: `#wtfact #actuallytrue #funfact #didyouknow #facts`

## Campaigns

### Source carousel (`kampagnen/karussell-honig-1…5`)
3,000-year-old honey that is said to still be edible? 🍯 Swipe to see how bees pull it off. All sources are on the last slide.
Save it for your next small talk.
`#wtfact #actuallytrue #honey #science #funfact #didyouknow #bees #facts`

### True or fake? (story, `wahr-oder-fake-1-frage`, then `-2-aufloesung`)
Story 1: put the poll sticker on the dashed area, answers "A" and "B". No slogan on the question story.
Story 2 (a few hours later): the answer. The fake is always resolved in the same story set, never left standing.
Blank template for your own pairs: `vorlagen/wahr-oder-fake-frage`.

### Prove it! (story, `kampagnen/beweis-es-aufruf`)
Question sticker on the dashed area, sticker text: "Which fact don't you believe?"
Answer with `vorlagen/beweis-es-antwort`, question on top, result below. Always with a source in bio or in the post. If something turns out to be wrong, we say so just as openly.

### For real? (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
Cover: the story examples (`beispiele/story-…`) or `vorlagen/reel-titel-echt-jetzt`. End card: `kampagnen/reel-abspann` (2 seconds).
Structure, 15 to 25 seconds, Pickles example:
1. Hook (0–3 s): "In 1966 a dog found the World Cup trophy. For real?"
2. Explanation (3–15 s): trophy stolen in London just before the World Cup, a week later the collie Pickles found it on a walk.
3. Flash the source on screen (15–18 s).
4. End card with a spoken "And yes, it's actually true."

### Launch (`launch-ankuendigung`, then 7 days of `beispiele/post-…`, then `launch-woche-finale`)
Announcement:
WTFact?! is here. Starting today, one fact a day that makes you go "wait, what?". Each one backed by at least two sources.
And yes, it's actually true. Link in bio.
Finale after day 7:
7 days, 7 facts, and all of them are actually true. Which one surprised you most? Drop the number in the comments. 👇

### Science Jumper (`science-jumper-post`, `-story`)
New on WTFact: Science Jumper. 📚🔥 Jump over the flames, collect blue test tubes and unlock 16 gold facts you can only find in the game. How far can you get?
Play now: link in bio.
`#wtfact #sciencejumper #browsergame #funfact #gaming`

### Swiping (`kampagnen/wischen-post`, `-story`)
New on WTFact: swipe the facts. 👆 Love today's fact? Drag it up and to the right. Not your thing? Up and to the left. The next one shows up right away.
Try it: link in bio.
`#wtfact #funfact #didyouknow #facts #swipe`

### Monday vote (`kampagnen/abstimmung-montag-post`, `-story`, blank: `vorlagen/abstimmung-…`)
Every Monday. The list shows the facts of the last 7 days; fill the blank template with that week's titles (newest first, like on the site).
Monday is voting day! 🗳️ Which fact of the last 7 days was the best? The winner becomes fact of the week. Vote until midnight (German time): link in bio.
No slogan on vote graphics or in the caption (slogan rule 3). Add a link sticker to the homepage on the story.

### Fact of the week (`vorlagen/fakt-der-woche-…`, example `beispiele/fakt-der-woche-honey-forever-…`)
On Tuesdays, once the vote is decided. The honey example only shows the layout: always post the real winner from the homepage, never a made-up one.
You voted: 🏆 the fact of the week is "[title]". [One sentence about the fact.] Full fact + sources: link in bio.
And yes, it's actually true.

### New this week (`kampagnen/neu-diese-woche`)
On Sundays as a reminder for the vote. Shows the 7 newest facts tagged "NEW".
7 new facts in 7 days. Which one was your favourite? We vote tomorrow. 👀 Link in bio.

| Day | What |
|---|---|

| Monday | Vote (feed + story with link sticker, story again in the evening) |
| Tuesday | Fact of the week (feed + story) |
