## Please

- **Keep the graphic unchanged:** don't edit or crop numbers, text or the logo. The facts are checked, and a single word can make them wrong.
- **Credit us:** tag WTFact or write `wtfact.xyz` in the caption. Every graphic shows the address at the bottom, where the full fact and its sources live.
- **Keep “AI-generated” in the bottom right.** It's our transparency label.
- **Don't present them as your own** and don't sell them.
- **If you get something from us in return** (money, products, a return post), label the post as an ad as the law in your country requires. Simply sharing is not an ad.
