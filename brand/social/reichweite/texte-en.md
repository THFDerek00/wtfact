# WTFact reach pack: copy (English)

Complements the social kit (`brand/social/kit/`). Every graphic is generated by `brand/build-social-reichweite.mjs` from the fact database, and every fact has at least 2 sources on the site. Big numbers are checked against the fact's title by the script.

Rules as in the kit: always write “WTFact?!” with “?!”. Slogan only next to sourced facts, never on quiz questions, polls or duels. Sources always one tap away (link in bio). The “AI-generated” label stays on every graphic.

### Duel (story, `duell/<n>-…`)

### Science Jumper (`science-jumper/…`)
